Site Plan: SharpCuts Barbing Salon

Site Name

SharpCuts Barbing Salon — chosen because it conveys precision, style, and professionalism, which are key qualities customers look for in a salon. Optional domain: sharpcutsalon.com

Site Purpose

The site will showcase SharpCuts Barbing Salon’s services, including haircuts, beard grooming, and styling. It will provide booking options, display price lists, and highlight customer testimonials. The site will also feature a gallery of styles and a contact page for inquiries.

Scenarios

  • How can I book an appointment online?
  • What are the latest haircut styles offered at SharpCuts?

Color Scheme

Primary Color: Navy Blue (#1e3d59) — used for headings and navigation.
Secondary Color: Clean White (#fdfdfd) — used for background.
Accent Color: Gold (#daa520) — used for buttons and highlights.

Typography

Headings: Tahoma, sans-serif
Body: Arial, sans-serif

Wireframe

Desktop View (Large Screen):

  • Header: Logo on the left, navigation menu on the right (Home | Services | Gallery | Booking | Contact).
  • Hero Section: Full-width image of a stylish haircut, overlay text “Look Sharp, Feel Confident,” and a “Book Now” button.
  • Main Content: Left (2/3 width) — service descriptions with icons (Haircuts, Beard Grooming, Styling). Right (1/3 width) — price list and quick booking form.
  • Gallery Section: Grid of haircut photos.
  • Footer: Contact info, social media links, and opening hours.

Mobile View (Small Screen):

  • Header: Logo centered, hamburger menu for navigation.
  • Hero Section: Stacked image banner with overlay text and “Book Now” button.
  • Main Content: Services stacked vertically, followed by price list.
  • Gallery: Single-column scrollable images.
  • Footer: Contact info stacked with social icons below.
